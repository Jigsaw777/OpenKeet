# Challenge Mode: practice with feedback

**Practice the hard questions. Learn from every answer.**

Practice rounds use your interview pack's questions, follow up on what you actually said, and give feedback you can check. Everything works offline with typed answers. The feedback is for learning; it scores the answer, not you, and never predicts hiring.

## A round

1. Open **Practice**, choose a pack and a kind: technical fundamentals, system design, behavioral (your stories) or everything.
2. Pick difficulty and number of questions. **Coach me after each answer** shows feedback right away; turn it off for interview-style feedback at the end.
3. Type your answer, or paste a transcript and fix recognition mistakes first. Feedback always uses the text you submit.
4. **Skip** keeps a question due for review. **End round** shows a summary.
5. **Save this explanation to my pack as a draft** adds your answer as a new draft. It never replaces an approved answer until you approve it in Prepare.

Questions are ordered by what's due, then weakest topics, then unpracticed ones, one per topic first. The round records the pack revision it started from.

## Follow-ups

Up to 2 follow-ups per question (configurable 0–4), chosen deterministically:

1. Linked questions from the pack, scored by how well they cover key points you missed and words you used.
2. Generated prompts bound to the same question: *What was your own role in that?* when you only said "we", *How did you know it worked?* when there's no result, *What would you do differently next time?* when there's no reflection, or *You didn't mention …* for a missed key point.

A question is never asked twice in a round. The same answer always gets the same follow-up.

## Feedback rubric

Scale per criterion: 0 missing, 1 mostly absent, 2 partial, 3 good, 4 strong, or *Not assessed*.

| Criterion | Offline method |
| --- | --- |
| Relevance | Overlap with the question's words and topic |
| Key points covered | Share of outline points found (cue phrases for template outlines, word overlap for community outlines) |
| Reasoning | Reasons and trade-offs ("because", "however", "depends", …) |
| Structure | Sentences and ordering words ("first", "then", "for example") |
| Technical correctness | **Not assessed offline.** Keyword checks can't verify facts. |
| Your own experience (personal questions) | "I" statements that match facts you confirmed |

Answers under 8 words score 0. The average maps to *Needs practice* (0–1.4), *Developing* (1.5–2.4), *Comfortable* (2.5–3.4) or *Strong* (3.5–4). Feedback lists what you covered (with your own words quoted), one thing to strengthen, a better structure, a follow-up, and this limitation: it can miss correct answers phrased differently and does not check facts.

Accent, voice, speed, age and other personal traits are never scored. Self-ratings are stored but never change scores.

## Readiness map

Per topic: number of scored answers, recent performance (mean of the last 3), and confidence in the estimate: *Tentative* after 1 answer, *Low* for 2–3, *Medium* for 4–6, *Higher* after that. Review dates use a simple interval schedule, not FSRS: 1 day for needs practice, 2 for developing, 4 or 7 days for comfortable or strong, doubling with consecutive good answers up to 8×. Skips are due immediately. **Reset practice history** deletes attempts and readiness.

## Limits

## Answer by voice

**Answer by voice** records from your default microphone through the same local speech model as live sessions. It never answers automatically. **Stop and use what I said** puts the transcript in the answer box so you can fix recognition mistakes before submitting. The attempt is stored as a voice answer. Nothing is kept unless you submit. It's disabled during a live session, without a microphone, or before a speech model is set up.

## AI feedback (optional)

With an AI provider connected, **Get AI feedback** sends the question, its key points and your answer to that provider. The reply must follow the same 0–4 rubric. Unknown criteria and out-of-range scores are dropped, and nothing in the reply is run or trusted as an instruction. The AI's scores are shown beside the offline scores and labeled with the provider and model. Only its correctness score, which offline rules can't assess, counts toward the readiness map. AI feedback can be wrong, especially on facts, and says nothing about hiring.

Offline feedback quality depends on how specific a question's outline is.
